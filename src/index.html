What We Do

Cleaning Services Built for the Lowcountry

From a single driveway to a multi-level parking garage — we have the right pressure, water temperature, and technique for every surface.

Residential & Commercial Pressure Washing

Homes, storefronts, warehouses and industrial sites. We match the method to the surface — soft-wash for siding and roofs, high-pressure where it's safe — so everything comes clean without damage.

  • House siding, brick & stucco
  • Storefronts & commercial buildings
  • Decks, fences & patios

Drone Cleaning

Our Lucid Bots Sherpa drone reaches surfaces ladders can't — high walls, signage and rooftops — safely from the ground. Soft-wash at 300 PSI or high-pressure up to 4,500 PSI, covering over 300 sq ft per minute.

  • Multi-story buildings & rooftops
  • Tall signage & hard-to-reach walls
  • No scaffolding, no risk
